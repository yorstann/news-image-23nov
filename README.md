# The World Today — Monday 23 November 2026

One procedurally drawn image summing up the day's world news, generated by `main.ts` (Node, standard library only — its own rasterizer, bitmap font and PNG encoder) and written to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` as a 1024×1024 broadsheet front page.

The front page leads with the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya — no deal after the EU and others refused any text without fossil-fuel phase-out language, Türkiye's final draft omitting the words "fossil fuels" — illustrated by a cracked Earth, a thermometer running hot, and the empty chairs of a summit hall that found no deal. Around it: the widening US–Iran war, with Houthi strikes on a Saudi oil refinery and an Iran strike wounding two in Bahrain; at least 14 people missing after a Russian drone strike in the Kyiv region; Javokhir Sindarov taking White in Game 1 as the 2026 FIDE World Championship opens; South Korea's education minister resigning over an AI exam-cheating scandal; and seven dead with 50 missing after a factory fire in Indonesia.

Run it with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
